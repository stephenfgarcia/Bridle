## Spec

What: Illustrative checks: generic section catalog, stress utilization, deflection and drift limits. Why: the showcase of 2026-12-16 for the owner's pitch and thesis defense on 2027-01-01. Review station: yes (the full bar: a wrong number or a false statement fails the card). The foreman writes this card's acceptance criteria here before its build starts.

Foreman's note (2026-11-18): the generic section list this card's plan row names already exists in `packages/tasks` (card T2: `GENERIC_I_SECTION_DIMENSIONS`, `genericSection`). This card adds the checks to `packages/solver` and does not make a second catalog.

Acceptance criteria (foreman, 2026-11-18):
1. `@bridle/solver` exports `memberChecks(model, result, loadCaseId, options?)`, which returns for every member of kind frame without releases, from `memberValues`: (a) the largest tensile and the largest compressive normal stress over the member's stations and over the four corners of the section's bounding rectangle (half of `depthY` and `depthZ` from the member's axis), by sigma = N/A − My·z/Iy − Mz·y/Iz with tension positive, each with its position and corner; (b) the stress ratio, the largest absolute normal stress divided by the material's `fy` times an allowable factor (option, default 1.0); (c) for a member that is not vertical, the largest deflection measured from the straight line between its displaced ends, perpendicular to the member, the span (the member's length), the limit span/n (option n, default 360) and their ratio; (d) for a vertical member, the relative horizontal displacement of its two ends, its height, the limit height/n (option n, default 300) and their ratio. A member counts as vertical by the same rule `memberLocalAxes` uses. All values are in the model's units (kN/m² for stress, m for deflection); the function does not convert to MPa or mm.
2. `structureChecks(...)` returns the governing member and value for each of the three ratios, and whether each is at most 1.
3. The checks are illustrative and say so in the data and the README: the result carries a `kind: "illustrative"` marker and the list of what is not checked, which the README states in full: no buckling of members or plates, no lateral-torsional buckling, no shear or torsion stress, no interaction formula of a design code, no load or resistance factors beyond the one allowable factor, no connections, no fatigue, no serviceability criteria beyond the two limits. No design code is cited as the basis of a limit; the limits are parameters.
4. A deflection limit for a beam made of several members in a line (a span between supports modelled with intermediate nodes, as the seeded tasks do) is also offered: `spanDeflection(model, result, loadCaseId, memberIds, n)` takes the collinear members of one span in order, and measures the largest deflection from the line between the span's two displaced ends over the span's whole length. It refuses members that are not collinear and connected, with a typed result.
5. Tests against hand values, each with its tolerance stated in the test: a simply supported beam under a uniform load (sigma = M/W with W = Iy/(depthZ/2), at mid-span, tension at the bottom corner and compression at the top; the deflection ratio (5wL⁴/384EI)/(L/360)); a cantilever (tension at the top at the fixed end; deflection measured from the chord between the displaced ends, which for a cantilever is not the tip deflection: state and test what it is); a member under axial force and bending together (the two extreme stresses differ by 2M/W and their mean is N/A); biaxial bending (the governing corner and its value); a member with a roll angle of 90 degrees (the weak axis governs); a portal frame under a lateral load (the drift of each column against the solver's own displacements; the beam's deflection from the chord excludes the sway); a beam of two collinear members with a load across both (the span deflection equals the single-member value); a member whose `fy` or depth makes the ratio exactly 1 at a chosen load. The three seeded tasks' reference models are checked for sense in a test that does not import `packages/tasks` (rebuild the 7 m beam with the "I 400x180" properties typed into the test with their source) and the bending stress of that beam under 25 kN/m is recorded in the card for card T6.
6. The functions are pure and return plain data; the cost for 200 members is measured and recorded as a measurement; `pnpm check` is 7/7 and `bash tools/test/lanes.test.sh` passes.
Files: `packages/solver/`. Out of scope: any interface (W11), the stress reference inside `packages/tasks` (the platform lane adds it from this card's recorded value), truss members and releases (S5).

### The formulas and their sign reasoning

**Stress.** σ(y, z) = N/A − My·z/Iy − Mz·y/Iz, tension positive, (y, z) in local axes from the member's axis.
Derived from the convention of `memberValues`, not copied: `N` is positive in tension. `memberValues` defines My as
positive with tension on the −z side (its header derives My = −∫ z σ dA); for a stress linear in z this gives the term
−My·z/Iy, which is positive (tension) at a negative z. Likewise Mz is positive with tension on the −y side
(Mz = −∫ y σ dA), giving −Mz·y/Iz. Proved by tests on the simply supported beam: under gravity (roll 0, bending about local
y) the tension corner is at z = −depthZ/2, the bottom; under a load along −Y (bending about local z) it is at
y = −depthY/2, the side the beam bows towards; with a roll of 90 degrees under gravity the tension corner is 75 mm below
the axis in global Z and the weak axis governs; and for the beam drawn both ways with five roll angles under two load
directions (20 cases) the tension corner is on the side the load pushes towards. Eight cantilevers (along X, along Y,
skew, inclined, vertical upward and downward, with roll angles) under a tip force in a general direction have the tension
corner on the side away from the force at the fixed end.

**Where on the section.** The four corners of the bounding rectangle, y = ±depthY/2, z = ±depthZ/2 (criterion 1).

**Where along the member: exact, not at stations only.** The foreman's note 2 asked for enough positions with a stated
accuracy, or a bound. I did neither and something stronger: between two consecutive stations the stress of one corner is a
polynomial of degree two in x (N linear, My and Mz of degree two; every point load has stations), and its derivative
(dN/dx)/A − Vz·z/Iy − Vy·y/Iz is linear and known at both stations from the arrays of `memberValues` (dMy/dx = Vz,
dMz/dx = Vy; dN/dx from the two values of N). Where it changes sign between two stations, the zero and the stress there
are computed in closed form: step = h·s₀/(s₀ − s₁), σ = σ₀ + s₀·step/2. So the extremes are those of the whole length up
to rounding, for the combined stress of each corner (the largest |My| and |Mz| need not be at the same x, and with an
axial load that varies the largest stress is at neither). `memberValues` is called once per member, without `at`.
Tests: the mid-span stress with 6, 8 and 1000 stations (no station at mid-span) at 1e-12; a beam with uniform transverse
and axial loads, whose extremes are at L/2 ∓ p·Iy/(A·w·c), at 1e-12 with 5, 21 and 200 stations; a skew rolled member
with six loads, where 5, 21 and 1001 stations agree at 1e-10.

**Deflection of a member.** r(x) = d(x) − [d(0) + (x/L)·(d(L) − d(0))] in local axes; the value is the largest over the
stations of √(r_y² + r_z²), with its position and the two components. It is at stations only (the default 21, never fewer
than 5: the second review of PX-01.6 advised against fewer). It is never above the true largest value and below it by at
most h²·κ/8 (h the largest station spacing, κ the largest curvature √((My/EIy)² + (Mz/EIz)²)); reasoning: at the true
largest point x* the derivative of |r|² is zero, the nearest station is within h/2, and the component of r along r(x*)
falls by at most (h/2)²·max|r''|/2 with |r''| = curvature. The bound is tested on the cantilever with 5, 21 and 1001
stations.

**For a cantilever this is not the tip deflection** (foreman's note 3). It is the distance of the bent axis from the line
between the fixed end and the displaced tip: PL³/(9√3·EI) at L(1 − 1/√3) for a tip load, 1/(3√3) = 0.19245 of the tip
deflection; 0.019686·wL⁴/(EI) at 0.37 L for a uniform load, 0.1575 of the tip deflection. Stated in the README and tested.
No tip-relative alternative is offered, because no criterion asks for one; the README says the node displacements hold it.

**Drift.** For a vertical member (the rule of `memberLocalAxes`, applied to the unit vector `memberValues` returns:
hypot(x.x, x.y) ≤ `VERTICAL_TOLERANCE`): dX, dY = end node's minus start node's displacement along global X and Y, from
the result of `solve`; value = √(dX² + dY²); limit = member's length / n.

**Span deflection.** The walk goes through shared nodes whichever way each member is drawn (foreman's note 4); the chord is
between the displacements of the span's two end nodes; the part perpendicular to the span is taken, at the stations of all
members.

### Decisions

- **Option names.** The criterion says "option n"; there are two n, so the options are `deflectionDivisor` and
  `driftDivisor`, and the results carry `divisor` and `limit`.
- **Invalid options** (not a finite number above zero) are replaced by the defaults, and the values used are in the result.
- **`passes` is `ratio <= 1` with no tolerance**, on the computed number.
- **`tension` and `compression`** are the largest and the smallest σ; `tension.value` can be negative (a member in
  compression throughout) and is documented so. `largest` is the larger magnitude, and the ratio uses it.
- **Ties**: first position along the member, then the first corner in the order (−y, −z), (+y, −z), (−y, +z), (+y, +z).
  With uniaxial bending the corner reported is one of two with the same stress.
- **`notChecked`** is a copy of `NOT_CHECKED`, ten codes: `member-buckling`, `plate-buckling`,
  `lateral-torsional-buckling`, `shear-stress`, `torsion-stress`, `design-code-interaction`,
  `load-and-resistance-factors`, `connections`, `fatigue`, `other-serviceability`. The criterion's eight items are ten
  codes because "members or plates" and "shear or torsion" are split.
- **`skipped`** lists the members without checks with a reason (`truss`, `release`, `no-values`), so the interface can say
  why a member has no colour.
- **`governingChecks`** is an addition to the criteria: it lets the interface call `memberChecks` once and derive the
  governing values without computing everything twice. `structureChecks` is exactly `governingChecks(memberChecks(...))`.
- **`spanDeflection` allows a span in any direction** and does not look at supports.

### Deviations from the criteria

1. Criterion 1(a) says "over the member's stations". The stress extremes are over the member's whole length (see above),
   which contains the stations. The reported `x` may therefore be between stations.
2. `spanDeflection` has a sixth, optional parameter `stations`, and its `n` has the default 360.
3. `governingChecks` is exported besides the three functions the criteria name.

### For the tester and the reviewer

- The checks of a member in a multi-member beam are per member: `deflection` of A-C is from A-C's own chord (about a
  quarter of the span's deflection for a half). The interface must use `spanDeflection` for the seeded beams.
- The deflection value is at stations; the stress value is not.
- The corner stress of a section that does not reach its bounding rectangle's corners (a tube) is not a stress of the
  section when both moments act; the README says so. The generic I-sections reach their corners.
- The time for 200 members is a measurement (README, "Cost"): `memberChecks` median 4.4 ms of 20 runs after 3 untimed
  (smallest 3.8, largest 9.1), Apple M4 Pro, macOS, Node v22.23.4, 2026-11-18, inside the test runner. The suite asserts
  under 1 second only.

### Observations (advisory, none fails the card)

For W11 (utilization colouring and results panel):

1. `packages/solver/src/memberChecks.ts:439`: the vertical rule is a cliff, as the spec asks, and the two measures are not comparable. The same 4 m cantilever column under 3 kN at its top: lean 0, 5e-10 and 1e-9 give `drift` 3.628e-3 m, ratio 0.2721; lean 2e-9 and 1 in 1000 give `deflection` 6.966e-4 m, ratio 0.0627, and no drift. A column that is not exactly vertical is coloured 4.3 times lower and is absent from the governing drift. Nodes drawn on a grid are exactly vertical, so this is not an ordinary case; the README (line 889) says an inclined member gets the chord measure but does not say that a leaning column then has no drift. Smallest remedy: one sentence there, and W11 shows which measure a member has (`MemberCheck.vertical`).
2. `packages/solver/src/memberChecks.ts:70-81`: second-order effects (P-delta) have no code in `NOT_CHECKED`. The README covers them only by reference to "Theory and limits" (lines 787-788: "small displacements"), and the interface translates the codes, so the panel will not say it. A steel designer expects it behind a column's figure. Suggest a code `second-order-effects` (an addition to the criterion's eight items), or a fixed sentence in W11. Also not said anywhere, and worth one line in the panel: the stress is first yield at the extreme fibre (no plastic resistance, no section classification, no net section), and the deflection is the total of the one load case as modelled (no combinations, no split of permanent and variable load, no pre-camber).
3. Per-member `deflection` for the seeded beams (several members per span) is from each piece's own chord: 0.2152 of the span's mid-span value for a half (README: "between 0.2 and 0.3", right; the card's and one test title's "about a quarter" is loose). W11 must colour such beams from `spanDeflection`, and `governingChecks().deflection` is of single members only (the README says so).
4. `spanDeflection` does not look at supports: the eight members of a two-span beam given as one list are accepted and measured from the chord over both spans (ratio 0.069 against 0.138 per span). The README says so (lines 936-937); W11 must pass one span at a time.
5. A cantilever's `deflection` is 0.19245 (tip load) or 0.15749 (uniform load) of its tip deflection, as the README states; confirmed from the closed forms. A panel that labels it "deflection" without "from the chord" would mislead.
6. The deflection values are at stations. The bounding-rectangle corners: for a round tube under two moments the corner value overstates the bending stress by up to √2 (when the two moments' stresses are equal); the README says "above every stress of the section" without the size. 9. `drift` is the whole relative horizontal displacement of the member's two nodes, including what a rotation of the structure below contributes. It is described exactly so; it is not a storey distortion.
