# PX-02.2 3D viewport

## Spec

Pitch card W2 of `docs/plan/pitch-plan.md` (web lane; apps/web), approved by the owner with the plan on 2026-11-18. What: 3D viewport: grid, camera, nodes, members and supports drawn from a model. Why: the showcase of 2026-12-16 for the owner's pitch and thesis defense on 2027-01-01. Review station: no (the pitch process: one agent builds and tests; `pnpm check` green before the commit). The foreman writes this card's acceptance criteria here before its build starts.

Acceptance criteria (foreman, 2026-11-18):
1. `apps/web` depends on `@bridle/model` (`workspace:*`). The viewport of `/workspace` draws a `Model`: each node as a small marker, each member as a line or thin cylinder between its nodes, each support as a glyph that tells a fixed support, a pinned support and a roller apart (decided from the six restraint flags; any other combination gets a generic glyph). The model's Z axis is up on screen.
2. Camera: orbit, pan and zoom with the mouse and trackpad; a "Fit" control that frames the whole model; three view presets (3D, front looking along +Y at the X-Z plane, top looking down the Z axis); a ground grid and a small axis indicator that shows X, Y and Z.
3. Until the model store exists (W3), `/workspace` shows the portal frame fixture of `@bridle/model`; a small switch in the side panel changes between the three fixtures (beam, portal frame, truss).
4. Hovering highlights a node or member and clicking selects it; the side panel then shows the selected item's kind, id and its data (coordinates for a node; start, end, section and material for a member), read-only, and "Nothing is selected" otherwise. The panel always shows the counts of nodes, members and supports of the model on screen.
5. The canvas stays client-only; `pnpm --filter web build` succeeds; the page shows no hydration error in the browser console.
6. Tests: a unit test of the pure function that turns a `Model` into what is drawn (positions, the counts, the support glyph kind for each fixture's supports); `pnpm e2e:smoke` extended to check that `/workspace` has a canvas and that the side panel shows the portal frame's counts and changes them when the fixture is switched; `pnpm check` is 7/7. One screenshot of `/workspace` at 1280 by 720 for the foreman.
7. Housekeeping left by W1: `tools/test/i18n.test.sh` is updated so that it passes with the present catalog and smoke tests; the sentences of `docs/ops/local-dev.md` and `docs/ops/i18n.md` that still describe `apps/web` as one page with no stylesheet and two strings are corrected; `tools/test/web.test.sh` (its file fixture and smoke counts) is kept passing.
8. Every user-facing string is in the catalog of `packages/i18n`.
Files: `apps/web/`, `e2e/`, `packages/i18n/messages/`, `pnpm-lock.yaml`, `tools/test/web.test.sh`, `tools/test/i18n.test.sh`, `docs/ops/local-dev.md`, `docs/ops/i18n.md`. Out of scope: editing the model (W4 onwards), the store and undo (W3), loads and results.

## Build

What was built, by acceptance criterion:

1. `apps/web` depends on `@bridle/model` (`workspace:*`; `next.config.mjs` lists it in `transpilePackages`, because the package exports TypeScript source). `/workspace` draws a `Model`: every node as a round marker 10 px across, every member as a line 2.5 px wide between its nodes, every support as a glyph under its node, drawn at 34 px per glyph unit (a glyph is at most 1.3 units wide). Markers and glyphs are rescaled on each drawn frame, so they keep their size on screen at any zoom. The glyph comes from the six restraint flags (`supportGlyph` in `scene.ts`): `fixed` (all six restrained) is a block; `pinned` (the three translations restrained, at least one rotation free) is a pyramid on a plate, a triangle on a line when seen from the front; `roller` (Z restrained, at least one of X and Y free, no rotation restrained) is a pyramid on two rollers on a plate; any other combination is `generic`, the edges of an octahedron around the node. For the fixtures: the beam has a pin and a roller, the portal frame two fixed supports, the truss a pin at `n1`, a roller at `n3` and the generic glyph at `n2`, `n4`, `n5` (held along Y only). The model's Z axis is up on screen.
2. Camera: drei's `OrbitControls` (drag to orbit; right-drag, or drag with Shift, Ctrl or the Command key, to pan; wheel or pinch to zoom; no damping). A toolbar in the viewport has four buttons: Fit (frames the whole model from the direction the camera has) and the views 3D, Front (looks along +Y at the X-Z plane, +X to the right) and Top (looks down the Z axis, +X to the right, +Y up). A ground grid lies on the plane Z = 0 (1 m cells, a major line every 5 m, sized to the model's plan with a margin) and drei's `GizmoViewport` in the bottom right corner shows the X, Y and Z axes in the `--viz-axis-*` colours.
3. Until the model store exists, `/workspace` shows the portal frame; a labelled `<select>` "Example model" in the side panel changes between the beam, the portal frame and the truss. A change clears the hover and the selection and frames the new model from the direction the camera has.
4. Hovering highlights the node or member under the pointer (the `--canvas-hover` colour; the cursor becomes a pointing hand) and a click selects it (`--canvas-selection`). The side panel then shows, read-only: for a node its kind, id and X, Y, Z in m; for a member its kind, id, start node, end node, section and material (name and id, as `I 300x150 (s1)`). Otherwise it shows "Nothing is selected.". A click on nothing clears the selection; a press and release more than 2 px apart is a camera drag and selects nothing. The panel always shows the counts of nodes, members and supports of the model on screen (the lengths of the model's lists).
5. The canvas stays client-only: `Viewport.tsx` loads `ViewportCanvas.tsx` with `next/dynamic` and `ssr: false` inside a client component. In a browser without WebGL the viewport area shows "The 3D view could not start in this browser." in place of the canvas and its toolbar.

Decisions and their reasons:
- Z up is set in one place, `SCENE_UP` in `camera.ts`. The canvas gives it to its camera (`camera={{ up }}` in `ViewportCanvas.tsx`), `OrbitControls` reads the up vector from the camera, `placeCamera` computes with it, and the grid is built on the plane Z = 0 by `gridLines` (no rotated `gridHelper`).
- One perspective camera. The 3D view has a 40 degree field of view. Front and Top use half a degree from far away (more than 600 m for the portal frame), which is close to a parallel projection: in the top view a column covers its base, and in the front view the ground is a line. Orbiting keeps the field of view of the last preset, until 3D is pressed. An orthographic camera was not used: one camera and one set of controls was the smaller change.
- Fit is computed from the eight corners of the model's bounding box (`placeCamera`), with 35 % free room, not from a bounding sphere, so a flat model fills more of the picture. The camera jumps; the move is not animated.
- Drawing: every member is in one `LineSegments2` (drei `Line` with `segments`) and every node in one `InstancedMesh`, so members and nodes are two objects whatever the size of the model; the hovered and the selected item are drawn over them as one more object each. Supports are a few small meshes per support. The canvas renders on demand (`frameloop="demand"`): a frame is drawn when the camera moves or React commits a change, and no React state is set per frame. Hover writes to the store only when the hovered item changes. The frame rate with 200 members was not measured.
- Picking: the index of the line segment under the pointer is the index of the member (6 px of tolerance around a member); the instance id is the index of the node; a node in front of a member wins. Support glyphs and the grid are not picked.
- `Viewport` takes `model` and an optional `frameKey`. The camera frames the model when the viewport mounts and when `frameKey` changes (the workspace passes the fixture id); a new `model` under the same key is drawn where the camera stands, so an edit (W4) will not move the camera.
- Colours come from the `--canvas-*` custom properties, read once when the canvas mounts: member, node, selection, hover, the two grid colours, `--canvas-support` for the outline of a glyph and `--canvas-grid-major` for its faces (the tokens have no fill colour for a support). Sizes (2.5 px members, 10 px nodes, 34 px glyph unit) are constants in `ModelScene.tsx`; the token `--canvas-member-width` (1.5 px) is not used: 2.5 px was chosen so that members stand out against the grid.
- The client components import `t` from `apps/web/src/i18n.ts`, so the catalog lookup now runs in the browser for `/workspace` (15.6 kB route, 118 kB first load in the production build).
- The viewport surface carries `data-hover` (`member:b1`, `node:n2` or empty) and `data-testid="viewport-surface"`: the browser test reads what the pointer is over from it.
- Two test scripts gained a helper `existing`: their file lists come from git's index, which still holds a file deleted in the tree until the deletion is committed, and the scripts would have handed the two removed files to `tar` and `shasum`.
