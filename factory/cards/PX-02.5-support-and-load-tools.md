# PX-02.5 Support and load tools

## Spec

Pitch card W5 of `docs/plan/pitch-plan.md` (web lane; apps/web), approved by the owner with the plan on 2026-11-18. What: Supports and loads: tools and glyphs. Why: the showcase of 2026-12-16 for the owner's pitch and thesis defense on 2027-01-01. Review station: no (the pitch process: one agent builds and tests; `pnpm check` green before the commit). The foreman writes this card's acceptance criteria here before its build starts.

Acceptance criteria (foreman, 2026-11-18):
1. Support tool: the palette gains a Support tool with a choice of Fixed (all six restrained), Pinned (the three translations) and Roller (Z held and the out-of-plane translation held, free along the member line of a planar frame: for the X-Z work plane that is uz and uy restrained and ux free; the choice names say what they restrain). A click on a node sets that support (one undo step); a click on a node that already has the same support removes it. The side panel shows, for a selected node, six checkboxes for ux, uy, uz, rx, ry, rz that edit its support directly, so any combination can be set.
2. Planar helper: because a frame drawn in the X-Z plane is unstable out of plane unless restrained, the Model section of the side panel offers "Restrain out of plane" which, for a model whose nodes all lie in one X-Z plane, adds to every node the restraints uy, rx and rz as one undo step, and says so; the supports it creates draw as a small unobtrusive mark, not as the large glyphs of real supports.
3. Load tool for nodes: a Load tool with a small form (load case, direction among X, Y, Z in global axes, value in kN, or a moment about X, Y, Z in kN·m); a click on a node adds that nodal load (one undo step). Nodal loads draw as arrows at the node, pointing the way the force acts, with the value and unit beside them; moments draw as a curved arrow or a double-headed arrow with the value.
4. Member loads: with a member selected, the side panel offers "Add uniform load" (direction, global or local axes, value in kN/m) and "Add point load" (direction, axes, value in kN, position in m from the start node, with the member's length shown). Uniform loads draw as a row of arrows along the member with the value; point loads as one arrow at the position.
5. The side panel lists the loads of the selected node or member, each editable (value, direction, position) and removable, and a Loads section lists the load cases with the number of loads in each, lets the user add a load case and choose the active one; the viewport shows the loads of the active load case only.
6. Arrow sizes are readable at every zoom (a fixed length on screen or a length relative to the model's size, stated in the Build section), do not hide the members, and use a colour from the tokens distinct from members and supports. Arrow direction follows the sign: a load of −10 kN in Z points down.
7. What the store refuses leaves the model unchanged and shows a short message, as in W4.
8. Tests: unit tests of the pure parts (which restraints each support choice sets; the out-of-plane helper and when it refuses; what is drawn for each load type: positions, directions, labels); one browser test that opens the portal frame example, adds a nodal load with the Load tool and a uniform load on the beam from the side panel, and reads both back from the side panel's lists; `pnpm check` is 7/7; `tools/test/web.test.sh` and `tools/test/i18n.test.sh` are kept passing. Two screenshots at 1280 by 720 for the foreman: the portal frame with a lateral nodal load and a beam uniform load in the 3D view, and the side panel with a member's loads listed.
9. Every user-facing string is in the catalog of `packages/i18n`.
Files: `apps/web/`, `e2e/`, `packages/i18n/messages/`, `tools/test/web.test.sh`, `tools/test/i18n.test.sh`, `docs/ops/local-dev.md` if a sentence there becomes untrue. Out of scope: the member property fields for section, material and releases (W6), the table editor (W7), analysis and results (W8 onwards), load combinations.

## Build

What was built, in layers. The pure parts import no React, no zustand and no three.js: `apps/web/src/components/viewport/loads.ts` (what is drawn for the loads of a load case) and `apps/web/src/components/tools/supportLoadTools.ts` (the rules of the Support tool, the Load tool, "Restrain out of plane" and the lines of a nodal load in the panel). `scene.ts` puts the drawn loads into the scene description (`buildScene(model, loadCaseId)`, field `loads`). `viewport/LoadGlyphs.tsx` only renders that description. `toolStore.ts` holds the Support tool's choice, the Load tool's form and the chosen load case; `toolActions.ts` runs the rules on the stores.

By acceptance criterion:

1. Support tool: the palette has Support (key S) and Load (key L) after Select, Node and Member. With Support active, a select beside the palette offers "Fixed (all six)", "Pinned (ux, uy, uz)" and "Roller (uy, uz; free along X)". A click on a node sets that support, replacing the one the node has, as one undo step, and selects the node; a click on a node whose support is exactly the chosen one removes it. A selected node shows six checkboxes ux, uy, uz, rx, ry, rz in Properties; each change is one undo step, and clearing the last one removes the support.
2. "Restrain out of plane" is a button in the Model section, with a line under it that says what it does. For a model whose nodes share one Y (within 1e-6 m) it adds uy, rx and rz to every node, keeping what each node had, as one undo step, and a line then says at how many nodes restraints were added. It changes nothing and says why for a model without a node, a model whose nodes do not share one Y, and a model where every node already has the three. A support that restrains only among uy, rx and rz is drawn as a small diamond outline around the node marker (16 px across, in the node colour): the new glyph kind `plane` of `supportGlyph`.
3. Load tool: with Load active, the toolbar shows the load case, the component ("Force X", "Force Y", "Force Z", "Moment about X", "Moment about Y", "Moment about Z", global axes) and the signed value (kN or kN·m). A click on a node adds that nodal load to the chosen load case (one undo step) and selects the node. A force is drawn as an arrow whose head touches the node and which points the way the force acts, with its magnitude and unit at its tail. A moment is drawn as three quarters of a circle around the node, in the plane it turns in, with an arrow head at its end (right-hand rule about the axis), with its magnitude and unit beside it.
4. Member loads: with a member selected, Properties has "Add uniform load" and "Add point load". Each opens a form (axes Global or Local, direction X, Y or Z, value; for a point load the position from the start node, which starts at the middle of the member, and a line with the member's length); "Add" adds the load to the active load case and closes the form, "Cancel" closes it. A uniform load is drawn as a row of arrows with their heads on the member and a line through their tails, with the value above the middle; a point load as one arrow at its position. Local axes come from `memberLocalAxes` of `@bridle/model`, with the member's roll angle.
5. Lists: Properties lists the loads of the selected node or member that are in the active load case, each as a small card with its fields (a nodal load: component and value per component that is not zero; a member load: axes, direction, value and, for a point load, position) and a Remove button; every edit is one undo step. A line says how many loads of the item are in other load cases. The Loads section lists the load cases as radio buttons with the number of loads in each, the checked one being the active one, and "Add load case" adds "Load case N" and makes it active. The viewport draws the loads of the active load case only.
6. Arrow sizes are fixed on screen and kept at every zoom (the groups are rescaled on each drawn frame, as the node markers and support glyphs are): a force arrow is 56 px long, the arrows of a uniform load 34 px, a moment's arc 46 px across. The arrows touch a member with their head only. The colour is the token `--viz-case-E` (#D55E00), which the members, supports, selection and hover do not use. The sign is in the direction: a load of -10 kN in Z has the direction [0, 0, -1] and the label "10 kN" (unit test).
7. Refusals leave the model and the undo steps as they were and show a message at the bottom left of the viewport, as in W4: a click of the Support or Load tool away from a node ("Click a node: this tool acts on nodes."), a load without a load case, a point load whose position is outside the member, and anything else the store refuses ("That change was not made."). The next edit that works clears the message.
8. Tests: see `## Test`.
9. Strings: 74 new messages (`workspace.tools.support`, `.load`, `.hintSupport`, `.hintLoad`, `workspace.support.*`, `workspace.planar.*`, `workspace.loadCases.*`, `workspace.loads.*`, three `workspace.notice.*`, `workspace.selection.length`); `en-XA.json` regenerated (162 messages). Decisions:
- The Roller choice is uy and uz restrained whatever the work plane is; it does not become another set on the X-Y work plane. Its name says what it restrains.
- `supportGlyph` changed in two ways. A roller is now also a support with uz, a free horizontal translation and exactly rx and rz restrained, so a roller keeps its glyph after "Restrain out of plane" (before: no rotation restrained). A support that restrains only among uy, rx and rz is `plane` (before: `generic`), so the truss fixture's three uy-only nodes are now small marks and no longer octahedra.
- One load case is "active": chosen in the Loads section or in the Load tool's form (the same state, `loadCaseId` of the tool store, read through `activeLoadCaseId`, which falls back to the model's first load case when the id is not in the model, for example after an undo). New loads from the tool and from the forms go into it, the viewport draws it, and the panel's lists show it. Loading another model drops the choice.
- A nodal load with several components that are not zero (a fixture or a file may hold one) is drawn as one symbol per component and listed as one card with one line per component; the Load tool itself makes loads with one component.
- Labels are HTML elements over the canvas, moved by a transform from the frame loop to where their anchors project (no React state per frame; text 12 px at every zoom; the pointer goes through them). drei's `Html` was tried first and dropped: on removing a load it logged "Attempted to synchronously unmount a root while React was already rendering" in the console.
- Label places: a force's value is centred beyond its tail; a uniform load's value beyond the middle of the tail line; a moment's value beside the open quarter of the arc. Nothing moves a label away from another label or from a member behind it.
- The arrows of a uniform load are about one twelfth of the model's largest dimension apart, with 2 to 16 spaces (13 arrows on the 6 m beam of the portal frame).
- When the scene has loads, Fit and the view buttons frame the model's box widened by 12 % of its largest dimension on every side (`roomForLoads`), so that arrows above the top member are not under the toolbar. A model without loads is framed as before.
- `apps/web/src/app/workspace/page.module.css`: the workspace no longer grows with its content (`flex: none`, one grid row of the fixed height). Before, a side panel taller than the window made the page and the canvas taller; with the new sections that happened as soon as a member was selected. The side panel now scrolls inside itself.
- The side panel has four sections: Model, Loads, Issues (before a block inside Model), Properties. A member's rows now include its length.
- Test hooks: `data-loads` on `viewport-surface` (the number of load symbols drawn); `load-label` with `data-load` (`nl1:fx`, `ul1`, `pl1`); `tool-options`, `support-choice`, `load-tool-case`, `load-tool-component`, `load-tool-value`; `restrain-out-of-plane`, `restrain-result` (with `data-outcome`); `load-cases`, `load-case` (with `data-case`, `data-active`), `load-case-count`, `add-load-case`; `node-support`, `restraint-ux` to `restraint-rz`; `node-loads`, `member-loads`, `load-row` (with `data-load`), `load-component`, `load-axes`, `load-direction`, `load-value`, `load-position`, `load-remove`, `load-list-empty`, `loads-elsewhere`; `add-uniform-load`, `add-point-load`, `new-load`, `new-load-axes`, `new-load-direction`, `new-load-value`, `new-load-position`, `new-load-length`, `new-load-add`, `new-load-cancel`.

Deviations and limits:
- Member loads are added from the side panel with the member selected, as the criterion says; the Load tool acts on nodes only.
- Not done, and outside the criteria: renaming or deleting a load case; moving a load to another load case from the panel; picking a load in the viewport; a partial uniform load (the model has none).
- Two loads at one node along the same axis are drawn on top of each other, and so are their labels. A label can lie over a member behind it or under the toolbar when the load is near the top of the view.
- A moment seen exactly edge-on (a moment about Z in the Front view) shows as a line with its value.
- A uniform load along its member (local x) is a row of arrows lying on the member, with its value above the member's middle; checked in a unit test (`alongMember`), not looked at in the browser.
- Changing the component of a nodal load line whose value is 0 is refused with "That change was not made." (nothing would change).
- The colour `--viz-case-E` has the same value as `--viz-diag-M2` and `--viz-axis-x` of the tokens.
- `bash tools/factory/lane-check.sh web main` was not run. `docs/ops/local-dev.md`, `e2e/`, `packages/i18n/messages/`, `tools/test/web.test.sh` and `tools/test/i18n.test.sh` are outside `apps/web` and in this card's Files list.
