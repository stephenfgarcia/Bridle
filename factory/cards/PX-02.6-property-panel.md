# PX-02.6 Property panel

## Spec

Pitch card W6 of `docs/plan/pitch-plan.md` (web lane; apps/web), approved by the owner with the plan on 2026-11-18. What: Property panel: section, material, releases. Why: the showcase of 2026-12-16 for the owner's pitch and thesis defense on 2027-01-01. Review station: no (the pitch process: one agent builds and tests; `pnpm check` green before the commit). The foreman writes this card's acceptance criteria here before its build starts.

Acceptance criteria (foreman, 2026-11-18):
1. Member properties: for a selected member the Properties section shows editable fields instead of read-only rows: the section and the material (each a select of the model's sections and materials), the roll angle in degrees, and the length (read-only). Each change is one undo step and triggers a new analysis through the existing live analysis. With several members selected the same fields apply to all of them (see criterion 4).
2. Sections and materials of the model: a "Sections and materials" area (in the Model section of the side panel or a dialog opened from it) lists the model's sections with their properties in the interface's units (A in cm², Iy, Iz and J in cm⁴, depths in mm) and its materials (E and G in GPa, fy in MPa, density in kg/m³), lets the user add a section from the generic I-section list of `@bridle/tasks` (`GENERIC_I_SECTION_DIMENSIONS`, `genericSection`; `apps/web` gains the dependency) and add a material from a short built-in list (structural steel S355 as in the fixtures, and S275), and remove a section or a material that no member uses. Stored values stay in the model's units (m², m⁴, kN/m²); the conversions are in one tested helper.
3. A small diagram or caption in the section's row or tooltip says which axis is which: Iy is the bending stiffness for loads along local z (the strong axis of an I-section drawn upright with roll 0), Iz for loads along local y.
4. Multi-selection: Shift-click adds to or removes from the selection, in the viewport, for nodes and members; the Properties section then shows the number of selected nodes and members, the fields common to the selected members (section, material, roll angle; a mixed value shows as "mixed"), and Delete removes all selected items as one undo step. Clicking empty space clears the selection.
5. Member end releases and the member kind (frame or truss) are shown read-only with the note that the analysis does not support them yet; the store commands exist but the interface does not offer to change them until the solver card S5 ships.
6. The viewport draws members with a line width or colour that distinguishes at least the sections in use (for example a legend of up to six colours from the tokens, by section), switchable on and off with a "Colour by section" control, off by default.
7. Tests: unit tests of the pure parts (unit conversions both ways; the mixed-value logic; the selection reducer with Shift; which sections and materials can be removed); a browser test that selects the portal's two columns with Shift-click, changes their section, sees the analysis status return to "Analysed" and a reaction value change; `pnpm check` is 7/7; `pnpm --filter web build` succeeds; `tools/test/web.test.sh` and `tools/test/i18n.test.sh` are kept passing. One screenshot at 1280 by 720 for the foreman: two members selected with their properties.
8. Every user-facing string is in the catalog of `packages/i18n`.
Files: `apps/web/`, `e2e/`, `packages/i18n/messages/`, `pnpm-lock.yaml`, `tools/test/web.test.sh`, `tools/test/i18n.test.sh`, `docs/ops/local-dev.md` if a sentence there becomes untrue. Out of scope: the table editor (W7), results in the viewport (W9 onwards), custom section entry by typing properties, releases and truss members.

## Build

What was built, in layers. The pure parts import no React, no zustand and no three.js and are unit-tested: `viewport/selection.ts` (the selection as a set and what a click does to it), `model/units.ts` (the unit conversions and the number format), `workspace/memberSummary.ts` (what selected members share, or "mixed"), `workspace/library.ts` (the rows of the sections and materials tables, what can be added and removed), `viewport/sectionColors.ts` (the legend and the colour of each member), and four new commands in `model/commands.ts`. The stores wire them (`viewport/viewportStore.ts`, `model/modelStore.ts`, `tools/toolActions.ts`); the components render them (`workspace/MemberProperties.tsx`, `workspace/LibraryDialog.tsx`, `viewport/SectionLegend.tsx`, and edits to `SidePanel.tsx`, `ResultsPanel.tsx`, `ModelScene.tsx`, `ToolLayer.tsx`, `ViewportCanvas.tsx`, `WorkspaceView.tsx`, `NumberField.tsx`). All paths below are under `apps/web/src/components/`.

By acceptance criterion:

1. Member properties: with one or several members selected, Properties shows rows with fields: Section and Material (selects of the model's sections and materials, each option as "name (id)"), Roll angle (°) (a `NumberField`: applied on Enter or on leaving the field), and read-only Length (m). A change runs `updateMembers` of the model store for every selected member as one undo step (`editProperties` in `tools/toolActions.ts`; a refusal leaves the notice "That change was not made."). The live analysis of PX-02.8 follows the store, so the model is analysed again; the browser test sees the status return to Analysed and a reaction change.
2. Sections and materials: the Model section has a "Sections and materials" button with a line "n sections, m materials" beside it; it opens a modal dialog (`<dialog>`, `LibraryDialog.tsx`). The dialog lists the sections (A in cm²; Iy, Iz and J in cm⁴; "Depth along z" and "Width along y" in mm; the number of members that use each) and the materials (E and G in GPa, fy in MPa, density in kg/m³, members), units in the column headers, numbers right-aligned in the monospace face, four significant digits with the digits before the decimal point in groups of three (8.4e-5 m⁴ prints as "8 400"). "Add section" adds the chosen entry of `GENERIC_I_SECTION_DIMENSIONS` through `genericSection` of `@bridle/tasks` (apps/web gained the dependency, `workspace:*`, and the package is in `transpilePackages`); "Add material" adds S355 (the values of the fixtures' material) or S275 (the same with fy 275 MPa). Remove is enabled for an entry no member uses; for one in use the button is disabled and its tooltip says by how many members. Each addition and removal is one undo step. Stored values stay in m², m⁴, m, kN/m², kg/m³; `model/units.ts` is the one file in apps/web that holds the factors (`toDisplay`, `fromDisplay`, `formatQuantity`).
3. Which axis is which: under the sections table, a small drawing of an upright I-section with its local y (to the right) and z (upward) axes, beside two sentences: Iy is the second moment of area about local y, the bending stiffness for loads along local z, the strong axis of an I-section drawn upright with roll angle 0; Iz is about local z, for loads along local y. A third line says the picture looks along the member with local x pointing at the viewer (x = y × z in `packages/model/src/geometry.ts`).
4. Multi-selection: the viewport store's `selection` is now a list of items (`Selection`), changed by `select(item | null)` (a plain click) and `toggle(item)` (a Shift-click), both through the pure `selectionAfterClick`. In the viewport a Shift-click on a node or a member adds it or takes it out, for nodes and members together; every selected item is highlighted. Properties shows a "Selected" row ("2 members", "1 node, 2 members") when more than one item is selected, the member fields for the selected members together (a select whose members differ shows a "mixed" entry that cannot be chosen; the roll field is empty with the placeholder "mixed"; the read-only rows print "mixed"), and a "Delete n items" button. Delete (button or key) removes every selected item with what depends on it as one undo step (`deleteItems`). A plain click on empty space clears the selection.
5. Member type ("Frame" or "Truss") and End releases ("None", or "Start: ry; end: none") are read-only rows, with the note "The member type and the end releases cannot be changed here yet: the analysis does not support truss members or end releases." The store commands exist (`updateMember`, `updateMembers` take `kind` and `releases`); no control calls them with those fields.
6. "Colour by section": a checkbox in a small box under the Undo and Redo buttons of the viewport, off when the page opens. On, each member is drawn in the colour of its section (3.5 px wide instead of 2.5 px) and the box lists the sections in use with their colour, name and number of members. The colours are six tokens, in this order: `--viz-case-W`, `--viz-case-T`, `--viz-case-L`, `--viz-case-Lr`, `--viz-diag-N`, `--viz-case-S`; none is the load colour `--viz-case-E` or the selection or hover colour. A seventh and later section in use is drawn in the plain member colour and listed together as "Other sections". The state is in the viewport store (`colorBySection`) and is not saved with the model.
7. Tests: see `## Test`.

Strings: 54 new messages (`workspace.member.*`, `workspace.library.*`, `workspace.sectionColors.*`, `workspace.selection.selected`, `.countNodes`, `.countMembers`, `.countBoth`, `.deleteMany`, `.multiHint`, `workspace.results.selectOne`), and `workspace.viewport.help` now mentions Shift-click; `en-XA.json` regenerated (270 messages). The axis letters of the drawing are messages too (`workspace.library.axisY`, `.axisZ`). `apps/web/i18n-allowlist.json` is still empty.

Decisions and deviations:
- A Shift-click on empty space keeps the selection (a Shift-click that misses a member should not lose the set); a plain click on empty space clears it, which is what criterion 4 asks.
- The sections-and-materials area is a dialog, not a part of the side panel: seven numeric columns do not fit the panel's width. While a dialog is open, the workspace's tool keys (the tool letters, Delete, Backspace, Escape) are not run, so Delete does not remove the selection behind the dialog; the undo and redo shortcuts still act.
- An entry whose name the model already has cannot be added again (the option is disabled and reads "name (in the model)"). So in the three example models "I 300x150" and "S355" cannot be added.
- `removeSection` and `removeMaterial` refuse an entry a member uses and an unknown id, nothing else: the only section of a model without members can be removed, and the Member tool then shows its existing notice "The model has no section or no material for a new member."
- A selected member is drawn in the selection colour over its section colour, so with "Colour by section" on, the section colour of a selected member is not visible until it is deselected.
- The colour of a section is its place among the sections in use, in the model's order. A section that comes into use before others in that order shifts the colours of those after it.
- Length of several members: the shared value when their lengths print alike, else "mixed".
- The pure module is `memberSummary.ts`, not `memberProperties.ts`: on a file system that ignores case it would collide with `MemberProperties.tsx`.
- `/workspace` in the production build went from 42.3 kB (PX-02.8) to 48.6 kB (first load 145 to 151 kB).

Found, not changed (outside this card's files):
- The fixtures' section "I 300x150" of `packages/model` has round properties (Iy 8 400 cm⁴, A 54 cm²) that differ from the generic "I 300x150" of `packages/tasks` computed from its dimensions (Iy 8 147 cm⁴). The two carry the same name; in the workspace's examples only the fixture's is present, because an entry with a name in the model cannot be added.
- In a script of mine a click sent immediately after a view button (Front) missed the item under it; with two animation frames between the view change and the click it hit. The smoke tests move the pointer and wait a frame before they click, so they do not meet this.

For later cards:
- W7 (table editor): the selection is `Selection` (a list of `ItemRef`) in `viewportStore`; use `select`, `toggle`, `singleItem`, `selectedIds`, `selectedItems`. Edits of several members go through `updateMembers`; several deletions through `deleteItems`; `removeSection` and `removeMaterial` exist. `formatQuantity`, `toDisplay` and `fromDisplay` of `model/units.ts` are the place for any unit shown in a table; `sectionRows` and `materialRows` of `workspace/library.ts` already give the tables' texts. A row of a table can select with `select` and extend with `toggle`.
- W9 (deflected shape and reactions): `ModelScene` takes `memberColors` (one colour per drawn member, or `null`); the selection highlight is drawn per selected item over the members. Results for "the selected item" should use `singleItem`.
- W11 (utilization colouring): it will colour members too. `memberColors` is the one channel: decide which of "Colour by section" and utilization wins (they cannot both be on), and reuse `SectionLegend`'s box position or replace it. The section palette uses load-case and diagram tokens (`--viz-case-*`, `--viz-diag-N`); the utilization ramp is `--viz-util-*`, so the two do not share a token.
- T4 (task mode): `MemberProperties` takes the model's whole section list; a task that limits the choice needs a prop for the allowed section ids (and a way to make Material, Roll angle and Delete read-only for fixed parts). `LibraryDialog` adds and removes sections freely: hide its button in task mode or pass it the allowed list. `sectionChoices(model)` is where the generic list is filtered.
