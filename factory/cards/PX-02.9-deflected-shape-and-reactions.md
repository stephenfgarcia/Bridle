# PX-02.9 Deflected shape and reactions

## Spec

Pitch card W9 of `docs/plan/pitch-plan.md` (web lane; apps/web), approved by the owner with the plan on 2026-11-18. What: Results: deflected shape with a scale control, reactions. Why: the showcase of 2026-12-16 for the owner's pitch and thesis defense on 2027-01-01. Review station: no (the pitch process: one agent builds and tests; `pnpm check` green before the commit). The foreman writes this card's acceptance criteria here before its build starts.

Acceptance criteria (foreman, 2026-11-18):
1. Deflected shape: when a result is shown for the model on screen, a "Deflected shape" control (on by default) draws the deflected structure from `deflectedShape` of `@bridle/solver` (21 stations per member) for the active load case, as a curve in a colour distinct from the undeformed members, sections and loads, with the undeformed structure kept visible and visually quieter while results are shown. Joints stay connected and the curves are smooth, because they come from the solver's own shape.
2. Scale: the displacements are multiplied by a scale shown on screen ("Scale ×N"). By default the scale is chosen automatically so that the largest displacement draws as a stated fraction of the model's size (for example one twentieth of its largest dimension), rounded to a tidy number (1, 2, 5 times a power of ten); a control lets the user set it (a slider or a number field, and a "True scale ×1" choice) and return to automatic. The automatic scale does not jump while the user edits a load slightly: say how in the Build section (for example recomputed only when it would change by more than a factor of two).
3. The largest displacement of the structure, in mm, and where it is (the member or node and the position), is shown in the Results section and marked in the viewport with a small label at the point on the deflected curve.
4. Reactions in the viewport: at each support, the reaction forces of the active load case are drawn as arrows (the three components separately, each pointing the way the support pushes on the structure, with its value in kN) and reaction moments as curved arrows with their value in kN·m, in a colour distinct from the applied loads; components of negligible size (the same rule as the Results section's formatting) are not drawn. A "Reactions" control switches them on and off.
5. Hovering or selecting a node shows its displacement (three translations in mm and three rotations in rad or mrad) in a small readout near the node or in the status line, in addition to the Results section.
6. Results follow the model: they use `shownResults` from the analysis store, are drawn at the node positions of the model the result belongs to, are faded when the result is stale, and are not drawn when there is no result (issues, unstable, failed). Switching the active load case switches the drawing.
7. Tests: unit tests of the pure parts (the automatic scale and its tidy rounding and stability rule; what is drawn for the deflected shape and for each reaction component from a known result; the largest displacement and its label; the mapping of a negligible component to nothing); a browser test that opens the portal frame, sees the deflected shape and reactions controls on, reads the scale and the largest displacement from the page, changes the load from the panel and sees the largest displacement change proportionally; `pnpm check` is 7/7; `pnpm --filter web build` succeeds; `tools/test/web.test.sh` and `tools/test/i18n.test.sh` are kept passing. Two screenshots at 1280 by 720 for the foreman: the portal frame under a lateral load in the Front view with the deflected shape and reactions, and the simply supported beam example under its load.
8. Every user-facing string is in the catalog of `packages/i18n`.
Files: `apps/web/`, `e2e/`, `packages/i18n/messages/`, `pnpm-lock.yaml` only if needed, `tools/test/web.test.sh`, `tools/test/i18n.test.sh`, `docs/ops/local-dev.md` if a sentence there becomes untrue. Out of scope: moment, shear and axial diagrams (W10), utilization colouring (W11), animation of the deflection.

## Build

Built on 2026-11-18 in the lane worktree `web`. One agent built and tested (pitch process).

What was built, in layers. The pure part is `apps/web/src/components/viewport/resultDrawing.ts` (no React, no three.js): the scale, the segments of the deflected shape, the largest displacement, the reaction symbols. `viewport/useResultDrawing.ts` calls it from the stores, in memos keyed on the solved model object, the result object, the load case, the scale and the two switches, on the main thread and not per frame. `viewport/ResultGlyphs.tsx` only renders that output inside the canvas and as HTML labels over it; `viewport/ResultsDisplay.tsx` is the control group; `analysis/NodeReadout.tsx` is the readout of a node.

By acceptance criterion:

1. Deflected shape: "Deflected shape" (a checkbox, on when the page opens) draws `deflectedShape` of `@bridle/solver` with 21 stations per member for the active load case, as one set of line segments 3 px wide in the colour `--plum-600`. While it is drawn, the undeformed members are 1.5 px wide in the node colour `--canvas-node` (2.5 px in the member colour otherwise); with "Colour by section" on they keep their section colours at 2.5 px (3.5 px otherwise). They are picked by the pointer as before (smoke test 10 selects the two columns after the frame is analysed, with the deflected shape on). The joints are connected because both members' curves end at the node's displaced position (unit test).
2. Scale: the toolbar shows "Scale ×N". Automatic by default: the scale aims at drawing the largest displacement one tenth of the model's largest dimension long (`AUTO_SCALE_FRACTION`), rounded to the nearest of 1, 2, 5 times a power of ten by ratio (`tidyScale`). A field takes a typed scale (a positive number, brought into 1e-4 to 1e9), "True scale ×1" sets 1, "Auto" returns to the automatic scale. The scale rule (`nextAutoScale`): the automatic scale is kept while the scale it would aim at is between half and twice the one in use; outside that it is rounded afresh; another opened model (the model store's frame key) or another load case starts afresh; while nothing moves the scale in use is kept. Because a tidy scale is within a factor of 1.59 of its target, the scale just chosen survives every change of the displacements by a factor below 1.26, and the drawn largest displacement stays between a twentieth and a fifth of the model's size. For the portal frame: 0.1 · 6 m / 4.891 mm = 123, so ×100; starting from 20 kN it stays ×100 while the load is between 12.3 and 49 kN, and it becomes ×50 at 60 kN (the unit test goes 20 to 25, back to 17, then 60 kN; the smoke test 20, 30, 300 kN).
3. Largest displacement: `largestDisplacement` is the longest displacement vector (three translations) over the nodes, the 21 stations of `memberValues` on every member, and the positions where one of the three global deflection components is extreme along a member (`extremes`). The Results section has a part "Largest displacement" with the value in mm (four significant digits) and "At node n2." or "On member b1, 1.200 m from node n2."; the viewport has a dot on the deflected curve at that point and the label "Largest 4.891 mm" above it (only while the deflected shape is drawn). For the simply supported beam it is 9.566 mm at 3 m, which is 5wL⁴/(384EI) (unit test at 12 decimals of a metre).
4. Reactions: `reactionGlyphs` gives one arrow per force component and one arc per moment component of every support, in the colour `--canvas-reaction` (the token that exists for it; the loads are `--viz-case-E`). An arrow points the way the support pushes the structure. A vertical component stands below the support symbol whatever its sign: head up at the support for an upward reaction, tail at the support and head down for a support that holds the structure down. A horizontal component stands on the side it comes from with its head at the node, as the load arrows do. A moment is an arc of 255 degrees around the node in the plane it turns in, with an arrow head, the sense by the right-hand rule about the component's axis (seen from the Front view, a reaction about −Y runs counter-clockwise). The value is beside each symbol, as the magnitude the Results section prints (kN, kN·m), without the sign. A component is not drawn when the Results section prints it as "0" (`formatNumber`: exactly zero, or at most 1e-9 of the largest reaction force, or moment, of the load case). "Reactions" (a checkbox, on when the page opens) switches them.
5. Node readout: the node under the pointer, or else the one selected node, shows a small card beside it with "Displacement of node n3", ux, uy, uz in mm and rx, ry, rz in rad, the numbers of the Results section (`displacementCells`). The card goes from the node towards the middle of the viewport, so it stays in the picture. The Results section still shows the displacement of the selected node.
6. Results follow the model: everything is read through `shownResults`; the drawing uses the model the result belongs to (`ResultDrawing.model`, the node positions of `shown.model`); while the result is stale the curve and the reaction symbols are drawn at 35 % opacity and the labels and the readout at 50 %; nothing is drawn when `shownResults` gives nothing or the result has no entry for the active load case, and the control group then says "No results to draw." The load case is `activeLoadCaseId(model, loadCaseId of the tool store)`, checked with `Object.hasOwn`.
7. Tests: see `## Test`.
8. Strings: 14 new messages (`workspace.display.*`, 9; `workspace.results.largest*`, 5); `en-XA.json` regenerated (284 messages). The labels of the reactions and of the readout reuse `workspace.loads.valueKN`, `.valueKNm`, `workspace.results.valueMm`, `.valueRad`, `.displacement` and `workspace.support.ux` to `.rz`.

Decisions:
- The automatic scale's target is one tenth of the model's largest dimension, not the twentieth the criterion gives as an example: with the tidy rounding and the factor of two a twentieth could draw the sway of the portal frame 10 px long at 1280 by 720. With a tenth it is drawn between a twentieth and a fifth.
- The colour of the deflected shape is `--plum-600`, a colour of the base palette. The tokens have no colour for a deflected shape, and every other candidate is in use or near one in use: the load colour, the selection and hover colours, the six section colours, the cividis colours and the red of utilization (card W11), the diagram colours (card W10). `docs/ux/tokens.css` says components use semantic, canvas and viz tokens only, so this is a deviation; the fix is a token `--canvas-deflection` in the token source, which is outside this card's files. The canvas reads the colours once, when it mounts, as before; the canvas themes other than light were not looked at.
- The largest displacement is the length of the displacement vector, not the largest single component. For the portal frame it is 4.891 mm on the beam 1.2 m from n2, not at the loaded node (ux of n2 is a little less): the beam's points there also move vertically. The value is the displacement of a real point, so it is not above the true largest one; it can be below it by what the vector's length gains between the positions looked at (the unit test bounds that at 0.1 % for the portal frame against 2001 points per member).
- The control group is one strip in the viewport's toolbar after the view buttons, not a box in the viewport: a first version sat under "Colour by section" and covered the top right joint of the portal frame in the Front view. At 1280 by 720 it fits on the toolbar's second row beside Fit and the views (measured: 549 px wide); with the Support or the Load tool active, whose options take the first row, it goes to a third row, which ends 29 px above the top of the portal frame in the Front view. The group's name "Results display" is its accessible name and its tooltip; it is not printed in the strip.
- The readout is a card beside the node, not a line in the status area: in the status area it covered the reactions of the left base in the Front view.
- The members step back only while the deflected shape is drawn, not while only the reactions are.
- The camera's Fit and view buttons leave the room of `roomForLoads` also when the model has supports and "Reactions" is on (before: only when loads are drawn), so that the arrows under the supports are in the picture.
- The arc of a reaction moment is 64 px across (a load moment: 46 px) and its gap is around the glyph's +X axis, so the two can be told apart at one node and the arrow head is clear of a horizontal and of a vertical arrow at the node.
- `SUPPORT_DEPTH_PX` in `resultDrawing.ts` repeats, in pixels, how far each support symbol of `ModelScene.tsx` reaches below its node. If the symbols change size, these numbers must follow.
- `largestDisplacementOf` (`useResultDrawing.ts`) keeps the last value computed, so the viewport and the Results section compute it once for the same model object, result object and load case.
- Test hooks: on `viewport-surface`, `data-results` (`none`, `stale`, `fresh`), `data-deflected` (members drawn deflected), `data-reactions` (reaction symbols drawn); `results-display` (with `data-available`), `show-deflected`, `show-reactions`, `deflection-scale` (with `data-scale`, `data-mode`: `auto` or `manual`), `scale-field`, `scale-auto`, `scale-true`, `results-display-none`; `result-labels`, `reaction-label` (with `data-result`, for example `n1:fx`), `largest-label`; `node-readout` (with `data-node`, `data-source`: `hover` or `selection`), `readout-value` (with `data-component`); `largest-displacement` (with `data-kind`: `node`, `member` or `none`, and `data-id`), `largest-displacement-value`, `largest-displacement-place`.

Deviations and limits:
- No slider: the scale is a number field with two buttons.
- The scale field shows at most four decimals (`NumberField`) and drops a typed number equal to the one it shows, so typing the automatic scale's own value does not switch to a scale set by hand.
- Rotations in the readout are in rad, as in the Results section, not in mrad.
- Nothing moves a label away from another label: the labels of two supports that are close on screen, or of a reaction and a load at one node, can overlap. In the 3D view the symbols at a fixed base are close together.
- A moment about an axis seen edge-on (a reaction about Z in the Front view) shows as a line with its value, as load moments do.
- The support symbols always point down (card PX-02.5), and so the vertical reaction is always drawn below the node, also for a structure supported from above.
- A truss member or a member with an end release would be drawn as a straight chord by `deflectedShape`; `solve` refuses such models today, so nothing is drawn for them.
- The deflected curve has 20 straight pieces per member. With member point loads the largest displacement is still looked for at the 21 stations and the component extremes only.
- Not looked at in the browser: the stale look (the fade while an answer is awaited lasts a few milliseconds here), a model with more than a few members, the dark and print canvas themes, widths below 1280 px.
- `bash tools/factory/lane-check.sh web main` was not run.
- No defect was found in `packages/model`, `packages/solver` or `packages/tasks`; none was edited.
